BusMi is a company that provides bus transportation services for institutions and associations. To make use of this service, customers must interact with an administrative office that is responsible for handling all stages of the booking process. The office can be contacted either by phone or in person at BusMi’s headquarters.
Regardless of the contact method, the booking process includes an initial phase in which the number and type of buses needed are identified. This is followed by a price negotiation phase (supported also by a staff member from the commercial department) and, once an agreement is reached, by the drafting and storage of the contract.
While the negotiation phase is mainly carried out manually, the selection of buses and the drafting of the contract are supported respectively by the resource management module and the contract management module of the ERP system used by the company. This ERP is implemented in a client–server architecture, with the client side running on the company’s internal PCs.
